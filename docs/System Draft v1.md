# **📘 Market Environment Interpreter (MEI)**

## **Finalized Draft — v1**

---

# **1️⃣ System Purpose**

The Market Environment Interpreter (MEI) is a two-timeframe analytical dashboard that:

* Displays core market metrics  
* Provides percentile and regime context  
* Delivers neutral economic interpretation  
* Integrates sentiment as positioning pressure  
* Maps conditional forward sensitivities (Option B framing)  
* Synthesizes environment summaries per timeframe

The system:

* Does not predict price  
* Does not generate trade signals  
* Does not recommend actions  
* Does not use directional bias

It interprets structure and conditions.

---

# **2️⃣ Structural Architecture**

## **Two Tabs**

### **A. Intraday Tab**

Structure-dominant, macro-aware  
Purpose: Define what kind of day it is structurally.

### **B. Swing Tab**

Macro-dominant, structure-confirmed  
Purpose: Define broader regime and financial backdrop.

---

# **3️⃣ Intraday Tab — Final MVP Structure**

## **Panel 1 — Intraday Volatility State**

Metrics:

* Current range percentile (30–60 day lookback)  
* VIX % change from open  
* Gap percentile (open vs prior close)

Function:  
Defines whether the day is normal, expansionary, compressed, or event-driven.

---

## **Panel 2 — Participation & Structure**

Metrics:

* Advance/Decline ratio  
* % index weight driving move (concentration proxy)  
* Volume vs 20-day average

Function:  
Defines structural integrity:

* Broad vs narrow  
* Conviction vs drift  
* Healthy vs fragile

---

## **Panel 3 — Cross-Asset Drivers**

Metrics:

* 10Y yield change (daily)  
* DXY change (daily)  
* Oil change (daily)

Function:  
Identifies whether intraday price action is:

* Rate-driven  
* Dollar-driven  
* Inflation-sensitive  
* Internally generated

---

## **Panel 4 — Event & Short-Term Positioning Context**

Metrics:

* Implied move for next major earnings (if relevant)  
* Days to major macro event (CPI/Fed/etc.)  
* Optional: dealer gamma regime (if available)

Function:  
Maps near-term event compression or sensitivity.

---

## **Intraday Conditional Sensitivity Section**

Structured framing:

* Identifies amplification conditions  
* Identifies what type of surprise would matter  
* No direction, only sensitivity

---

## **Intraday Summary (Generated After Full Analysis)**

Structure:

1. Dominant force  
2. Secondary modifier  
3. Sensitivity condition (if applicable)

Max 3–4 sentences.  
No prediction language.

---

# **4️⃣ Swing Tab — Final MVP Structure**

## **Panel 1 — Volatility Regime**

Metrics:

* VIX percentile (6–12 month)  
* ATR percentile (50-day)  
* Volatility trend (expanding/contracting)

Function:  
Defines risk appetite and compression/expansion phase.

---

## **Panel 2 — Financial Conditions Core**

Metrics:

* 10Y yield 3-month change  
* Real yield trend (if available)  
* DXY 3-month trend  
* Credit spread direction (tightening/widening)

Function:  
Defines tightening vs easing regime and macro pressure.

Primary backbone of swing analysis.

---

## **Panel 3 — Equity Structure Confirmation**

Metrics:

* % stocks above 50-day MA  
* Index concentration ratio (top 5 weight)  
* Sector rotation (cyclical vs defensive relative strength)

Function:  
Confirms whether macro pressure is visible internally.

---

## **Panel 4 — Sentiment & Positioning**

Metrics:

* Put/Call percentile (3–6 month)  
* Survey sentiment percentile  
* Volatility skew

Function:  
Identifies positioning stretch and asymmetry risk.

Sentiment modifies, never dominates.

---

## **Panel 5 — Catalyst & Event Context**

Metrics:

* Days to major macro event  
* Implied move for major index-heavy earnings

Function:  
Defines event-driven sensitivity within current regime.

---

## **Swing Conditional Sensitivity Section (Option B Framing)**

Structure:

* “Under current conditions…”  
* “Given compressed volatility…”  
* “If X were to occur…”

Maps potential reaction asymmetry.  
No directional claims.

---

## **Swing Summary (Generated After Full Analysis)**

Structure:

1. Dominant macro force  
2. Structural modifier  
3. Positioning modifier (if relevant)  
4. Sensitivity framing

Max 4 sentences.  
Neutral tone.

---

# **5️⃣ Interpretation Block Structure (Applies to Every Panel)**

Each metric cluster must follow:

A. Raw Metrics  
B. Context (percentile/regime comparison)  
C. Neutral Structural Interpretation (1–2 sentences)  
D. Optional Toggle:

* Why traders care  
* What this measures conceptually

---

# **6️⃣ Sentiment Integration Rules**

Sentiment:

* Functions as pressure gauge, not compass  
* Never appears first in summaries  
* Never overrides macro or structure  
* Always framed as asymmetry modifier

Example framing:  
“Optimistic positioning reduces margin for positive surprise.”

Never:  
“Market is too bullish.”

---

# **7️⃣ Language Discipline Rulebook (Condensed)**

The system must:

* Use “historically associated with…”  
* Use percentile framing when possible  
* Avoid deterministic language  
* Avoid directional prediction  
* Avoid emotional phrasing  
* Avoid hype tone  
* Avoid “will,” “crash,” “rally,” etc.

Conflict between signals must be acknowledged, not resolved artificially.

Tone:  
Calm. Clinical. Professional. Educational.

---

# **8️⃣ Summary Synthesis Logic**

Summaries must:

* Weight dominant force first  
* Identify secondary modifier  
* Identify amplification/sensitivity condition  
* Remain concise  
* Avoid repetition of raw metrics  
* Avoid prediction language

The summary is not a recap.  
It is a force-weighted integration.

---

# **9️⃣ Design Constraints**

* Intraday: 4 panels  
* Swing: 5 panels  
* Total metrics capped (\~27 core metrics)  
* No expansion without clear structural necessity  
* Minimal but deep

---

# **🔟 System Identity**

The MEI is:

* A structured reasoning scaffold  
* A macro \+ structure interpreter  
* A positioning pressure map  
* A volatility regime classifier  
* An educational cognitive amplifier

It is not:

* A trading bot  
* A forecasting engine  
* A narrative generator  
* A signal service
